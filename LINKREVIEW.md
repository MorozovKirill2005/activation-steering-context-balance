# LinkReview

- Here we collect all the works that may be useful for writing our paper
- We divide these works by topic in order to structure them

> [!NOTE]
> This review table will be updated, so it is not a final version.

| Topic | Title | Year | Authors | Paper | Code | Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Activation Steering | SHIFT: Steering Hidden Intermediates in Flow Transformers | 2026 | Nina Konovalova et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09213) | [GitHub](https://github.com/ControlGenAI/SHIFT) | The activation steering method for DiT |
| Dataset | NICO++: Towards Better Benchmarking for Domain Generalization | 2022 | Xingxuan Zhang et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08040) | [GitHub](https://github.com/xxgege/NICO-plus) | A benchmark for domain generalization with 80 hierarchical categories, 10 common and 10 unique contexts per category |
| Fairness | Fairness in Machine Learning: A Survey | 2020 | Simon Caton, Christian Haas | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04053) | - | Overview of methods for ensuring fairness in ML and metrics |
| Dataset | Change is Hard: A Closer Look at Subpopulation Shift | 2023 | Yuzhe Yang et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12254) | [GitHub](https://github.com/YyzHarry/SubpopBench) | It formalizes the subpopulation shift problem, adapts NICO++ for this task, and presents the SubpopBench benchmark with 20 algorithms and 12 datasets |
| Synthetic Data | Feedback-guided Data Synthesis for Imbalanced Classification | 2023 | Reyhane Askari Hemmat et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00158) | [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Feedback-guided-Data-Synthesis) | The method of balancing using synthetic data from LDM with feedback from the classifier |
| Activation Steering | Steering Language Models with Activation Engineering | 2023 | Alexander Matt Turner et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10248) | - | The ActAdd method, which calculates a steering vector based on intermediate activations using a single contrastive pair of prompts |
| Activation Steering | Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition | 2023 | Nina Panickssery et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06681) | - | The CAA method, which calculates the steering vector based on the averaging of the difference in activation of residual flows between positive and negative pairs of examples |
| Activation Steering | Controlling Language and Diffusion Models by Transporting Activations | 2024 | Pau Rodriguez et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23054) | - | The AcT method, which treats activation steering as an optimal transport problem and is used for LLM and T2I models |
| Activation Steering / Fairness | EquiSteer: Cross-Attention Steering Towards a Fairer Text-Guided Image Generation | 2026 | Tatiana Gaintseva et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01147) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Atmyre/EquiSteer) | The EquiSteer method, which uses activation steering in T2I models to combat demographic biases |
| Diffusion Models | Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models | 2020 | Jonathan Ho et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239) | [GitHub](https://github.com/hojonathanho/diffusion) | A basic article on diffusion that presents the DDPM model |
| Diffusion Models | Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models | 2020 | Jiaming Song et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02502) | [GitHub](https://github.com/ermongroup/ddim) | The DDIM method, which represents non‑Markovian diffusion to accelerate generation |
| Diffusion Models | High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models | 2022 | Robin Rombach et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10752) | [GitHub](https://github.com/CompVis/latent-diffusion) | The application of diffusion in the latent space of a pre-trained autoencoder |
| Diffusion Models | SDXL: Improving Latent Diffusion Models for High-Resolution Image Synthesis | 2023 | Dustin Podell et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.01952) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Stability-AI/generative-models) | LDM T2I model with an even larger U-Net |
| Diffusion Models | Scalable Diffusion Models with Transformers | 2022 | William Peebles, Saining Xie | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09748) | [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/DiT) | The DiT architecture, which replaces the U‑Net in diffusion models with a transformer |
| Diffusion Models | FLUX.1 Kontext: Flow Matching for In-Context Image Generation and Editing in Latent Space | 2025 | Black Forest Labs et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15742) | - | Generative flow matching model that unifies image generation and editing within a single architecture |
| Diffusion Models / Guidance | Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance | 2022 | Jonathan Ho, Tim Salimans | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598) | - | Introduces classifier-free guidance, where the model itself is trained to both condition on and ignore the class label |
| Activation Steering | CASteer: Steering Diffusion Models for Controllable Generation | 2025 | Tatiana Gaintseva et al. | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09630) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Atmyre/CASteer) | A training‑free method for controlling the generation of diffusion models via steering vectors in cross‑attention, allowing you to add, remove, replace, or change the style of concepts without retraining |
